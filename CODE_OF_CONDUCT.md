# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for contributors of every background and experience level.

Be constructive, assume good intent, discuss technical decisions with evidence, protect learner privacy, and accept feedback gracefully. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, or publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
